CLEAR ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions regarding data research.

Transparent explanations covering data cleaning methodology, deduplication protocols, and deliverable standards.

What types of messy data can you clean in Excel and Google Sheets? +
I resolve unparsed contact names, combined addresses, inconsistent telephone formatting, duplicate records, non-standard state abbreviations, unwanted whitespace, and broken character encodings. Datasets are returned normalized, categorized, and structured with frozen headers and active filters.
How do you detect and eliminate duplicate records in large datasets? +
I utilize multi-tier deduplication rules combining exact matches (e.g., unique email, tax ID, or corporate domain) and fuzzy matching logic (e.g., matching company names with slight legal suffix variations like 'Inc.' vs 'Incorporated'). Ambiguous records are flagged in a review column rather than blindly deleted.
Can you prepare datasets specifically for CRM import like HubSpot or Salesforce? +
Yes. I map your columns directly to your CRM's required property schema, ensuring separate First/Last names, standard ISO country codes, valid URL protocols, and deduplicated record IDs so your import executes with zero ingestion errors.
How do you find and extract data from public web directories and registries? +
I perform structured extraction from official government databases, state business registries, trade association directories, and licensed public registers. Every data point is cross-referenced for validity, ensuring clean, verifiable entries without corrupted characters or broken links.
How do you handle client data privacy and confidentiality? +
All client files are processed in secure, isolated environments and are permanently deleted following project delivery and client sign-off. I adhere to strict non-disclosure standards and never share, sell, or reuse client datasets.
What file formats and turnaround times do you offer? +
Deliverables are provided in your preferred format: Google Sheets (with live collaboration), Microsoft Excel (.xlsx), or UTF-8 encoded CSV / TSV files. Turnaround times typically range from 24 to 72 hours depending on dataset size and verification complexity.
